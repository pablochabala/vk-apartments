Proposed brand system and section-by-section wireframe for the VK Apartments site. Nothing has been coded yet. Pick a palette, check the motif, answer the open questions at the bottom, and I'll start building.

01 · Concept

Copper, malachite & home

Kitwe is the heart of the Copperbelt. The brand draws on two colours that come from the ground there: copper and malachite, the green copper ore. They sit on a warm limestone off-white. The motif comes from the property itself: the hexagonal paving in the courtyard and the ore-vein lines of a copper seam. This gives the site its own identity instead of borrowing the reference's arches and waves. The tone is warm, unhurried and lifestyle-first: a home that shapes how you live.

04 · Signature motif: "Hex & Vein"

Taken from the property

Ore-vein lines

Three parallel copper strokes. They act as section dividers, draw on with scroll (stroke-dashoffset) in Architecture, and run behind the manifesto.

Hex tile

A hexagon logo mark, hex-framed amenity icons, a faint hex lattice on dark sections, and a hex "close" morph in the menu.

Chamfered frame

Images use a clipped hex corner instead of rounded or arched corners. Reveals animate the clip-path from inset(…) to full. The map container uses the same shape.

07 · Wireframe

Section by section

00

Global chrome

Fixed header: hex logo · "Menu" · 057 060 9865 (tel:) · Book a call (tel: on touch, form modal on desktop). Header hides when you scroll down and returns when you scroll up. A floating copper pill, "Choose an apartment", sits bottom-right. A copper-ring cursor grows over links and images (pointer devices only). The full-screen menu opens as a malachite curtain with large staggered links, hover-swapped serif italics, phone and address. Esc closes it and focus is trapped while open. A grain overlay is fixed across the site.
01

Hero

Full-bleed video/still with a slow 1.15 → 1 scale. The headline reveals line by line: A home that / SHAPES HOW / YOU LIVE. Tagline "New apartments in Nkana East, Kitwe". A drawn-vein scroll indicator sits bottom-centre.

02

Manifesto

Your own rules OF MODERN LIFE. A big paragraph describes a day at VK: sunrise coffee at the kitchen island, the short drive into town, an afternoon by the pool, DStv and dinner with friends. Words colour in as you scroll. Vein lines run behind it.
03

Location

In the heart of NKANA EAST. Pinned on desktop: the text column stays fixed while three chamfered images parallax past at different speeds. Includes distance chips ("≈ 5 min to town centre", to be confirmed) and a Get directions magnetic button. On mobile it's stacked with no pin.
04

Marquee

Two rows of oversized outlined/filled type moving in opposite directions, separated by hex bullets: Mukuba Mall · ECL Mall · Copperbelt University · Kitwe Teaching Hospital · Nkana Golf Club · Freedom Park… Speed reacts to scroll velocity.
05

Your own territory

Amenity cards that scroll horizontally on desktop and become a 2-col grid on mobile. Each card has a hex icon, a title, a line of copy, and an image that zooms on hover. From your photos: swimming pool, secure parking, 24/7 security + electric fence, backup power, water supply, garden, DStv, Wi-Fi, air conditioning.
06

Interiors

Stylish, FOR REAL. Alternating split rows of image and text: kitchens (gloss cabinets, quartz tops), lighting (cove LEDs, pendants, backlit mirrors), bathrooms (marble-look porcelain, glass showers). Images reveal from inset clip-path to full, with inner parallax.
07

Architecture

A huge headline with vein lines drawn across it as you scroll (SVG stroke animation). Facade photo plus building description: single-storey modern blocks, grey render, hex-paved courtyard, landscaped beds.
08

Apartment types

Tabs → crossfading panel with floor plan (placeholder SVG), size m², bedrooms/baths, from ZMW —, and an Enquire button that preselects the unit in the contact form. Becomes a swipeable slider on mobile.
09

Gallery

Pinned horizontal scroll on desktop with a counter (03 / 12) and a progress vein line. On mobile it becomes an Embla swipe carousel with arrows (non-drag alternative). Clicking opens a lightbox.
10

How to rent / buy

Three large numbered steps: View → Apply → Move in. Each has a short description, and a vein line draws between them. Payment options are shown as a small list below.
11

Contact

Come home to NKANA EAST. Left: the form (name, phone, preferred unit, message) with labels, validation on blur, and a success state, sent via WhatsApp or email (your choice). Right: a huge tap-to-call number, the address, and a chamfered map embed (lazy-loaded, grayscale until hover) with an Open in Google Maps button.
12

Footer

Malachite background with a hex lattice. Contains links, phone, address, Get directions, socials (TikTok @vk.apartments), and an edge-to-edge "VK APARTMENTS" wordmark that rises on scroll. © VK Apartments.
